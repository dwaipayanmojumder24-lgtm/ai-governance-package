    4.1Everyday Rules

    4.1 Everyday Development: The 3 Rules You Need To Know

    Once governance is set up, what do you need to keep in mind while writing code? Just these 3 simple rules.

    Evidence base: baseline/policies/ | plugins/pull-request/ai_code_review_gate.py
    1Rule 1: Never Hardcode API KeysSecret Safety

    Always use environment variables for OpenAI, Anthropic, or Hugging Face tokens. The pre-commit scanner will stop you if you accidentally leave one in a file.

    2Rule 2: Have a Teammate Review AI CodeHuman Review

    If you use Copilot, Cursor, or Claude to generate code, at least 1 human engineer must approve your Pull Request before merging (ORG-CTL-IPR-002).

    3Rule 3: Keep Your Snapshot in GitSnapshot Sync

    Whenever you change your project settings, re-run the copilot or resolver so your effective-policy-snapshot.json stays in sync with your code.
