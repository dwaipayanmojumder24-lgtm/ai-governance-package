Executed Sample 4: HR Candidate Screening System (sample-hr-resume-screening)

High-Risk AI: What was caught as non-compliant, how it was analyzed, how it was fixed, and the resulting audit report.

High-Risk Recruitment & Employment AI

An AI tool that scans job applicant resumes, ranks candidates, and calculates fit scores.

Under the EU AI Act (Annex III) and global labor regulations, employment AI is classified as High-Risk (Tier 3). Without strict governance, this application could trigger multi-million dollar regulatory fines and devastating bias lawsuits.

What Was Caught as Non-Compliant (Before Governance)
VIOLATION: POL-SEC-01

Hardcoded API Secret in Code

The developer pasted an OpenAI secret key directly into resume_scanner.py. If pushed to GitHub, company cloud accounts would be compromised.

VIOLATION: POL-OVR-01

Illegal Autonomous Hiring Rejections

The code attempted to reject candidates automatically without a human recruiter review, violating mandatory EU AI Act human-oversight laws.

VIOLATION: POL-RET-01

Unmanaged Retention of Applicant PII

Candidate names, phone numbers, and home addresses were retained indefinitely with no deletion schedule.

How Governance Analyzed & Fixed It (After Governance)
ANALYSIS

The copilot scanned the files, detected candidate PII (email, phone, name) and ML libraries, and bound High-Risk Tier 3 rules (43 controls).

REMEDIATION 1: SECRET REMOVED

Moved API key to secure environment variables. Installed pre-commit secret shield to permanently prevent credential leaks.

REMEDIATION 2: HUMAN OVERSIGHT ENFORCED

Code modified to advisory mode only (PENDING_HUMAN_REVIEW). Recruiters make all final hiring decisions.

REMEDIATION 3: 1-YEAR GDPR RETENTION SEALED

Data Card configured with a strict 1-year data deletion schedule and Model Card bound to 85% fairness parity ratio.

Audit Result: 100% Compliant. 43 High-Risk controls active. Cryptographically sealed.

Slide 10 Executed Sample Projects • Agentic Sample 3

Executed Sample 5: Autonomous Financial Advisor (sample-financial-advisor)

Agentic AI: What was caught as non-compliant, how it was analyzed, how it was fixed, and the resulting audit report.

Autonomous Agent & Financial Tool Execution

An AI agent that monitors investment portfolios, recommends asset shifts, and executes bank fund transfers.

Because this AI can perform real-world financial transactions (`execute_fund_transfer`), an unconstrained agent could enter a runaway loop or execute unauthorized fund transfers if attacked by prompt injection.

What Was Caught as Non-Compliant (Before Governance)
VIOLATION: AGENT-OVERLAY-01

Unrestricted Tool Execution (Runaway Risk)

The agent had permissions to call bank transfer APIs autonomously without requiring any human dual-key authorization or spending cap.

VIOLATION: POL-RET-01

Missing SEC/FINRA Statutory Retention

Financial recommendations and transaction prompts lacked the mandatory 7-year statutory audit trail required by financial regulators.

VIOLATION: POL-SEC-03

Prompt Injection Vulnerability

External inputs were passed directly into financial execution tools with no recursion depth limits.

How Governance Analyzed & Fixed It (After Governance)
ANALYSIS

The copilot scanned code, detected tool-execution keywords (transfer_funds, requests.post), and bound the Autonomous Agent Overlay (46 controls).

REMEDIATION 1: DUAL-KEY SPENDING CAP

Enforced dual-key authorization: Any fund transfer over $500 is blocked automatically unless accompanied by a human approval token.

REMEDIATION 2: 7-YEAR STATUTORY AUDIT TRAIL

Data Card formally locked with SEC/FINRA 7-year audit retention rules and cryptographic prompt hashing.

REMEDIATION 3: RECURSION DEPTH BOUNDED

Agent restricted to maximum call depth ≤ 3, preventing runaway loops and prompt hijacking.

Audit Result: 100% Compliant. 46 Agent controls active. Dual-key protection enabled.

Slide 14 Executive Q&A Appendix • Management FAQ

Appendix: Senior Management & Boardroom Q&A

Direct, plain-English answers to the top 8 questions asked by Senior Executives, Directors, and Legal Counsel.

Q1

Will this slow down our software developers and delay AI product launches?

No, it actually accelerates delivery. Instead of engineers waiting 4 to 6 weeks for legal and security review committees to review 50-page questionnaires, the automated copilot inspects the code and sets up 100% compliant policies in 3 seconds. Development proceeds at full speed with continuous automated guardrails.

Q2

Is this compliant with international regulations like the EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, and ISO 42001?

Yes. The 16 baseline policies and 61 machine controls directly map to the mandatory articles of the EU AI Act (Articles 9–15: risk management, data governance, human oversight, transparency, accuracy), NIST AI RMF 1.0 (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage), and ISO/IEC 42001:2023.

Q3

Can our company customize the rules for our specific industry (e.g. Healthcare or Banking)?

Yes, 100%. All baseline policies are stored in plain, human-readable Markdown and YAML. Your risk team can adjust thresholds (e.g. accuracy limits, spending caps, retention periods) in minutes, or attach industry-specific overlays for financial, healthcare, or government requirements.

Q4

What if an emergency software patch must go live immediately and cannot meet every standard today?

The platform includes an official Exception Request Process. A project team can apply for a formal, time-bounded waiver (valid up to 90 days) requiring documented compensating safety controls and sign-off by the AI Safety Board. This provides necessary business flexibility without creating unmonitored loopholes.

Q5

Does this system send our proprietary code or company data to external cloud servers?

No. The entire governance platform runs 100% locally and within your own private corporate infrastructure (your developer laptops, internal servers, or private GitHub Actions runners). It never sends your code, intellectual property, or confidential customer data to third-party AI services.

Q6

How do we prove compliance when external regulators or auditors come to inspect us?

The platform generates an interactive Compliance Audit Report (governance-compliance-report.html) stamped with an immutable cryptographic SHA-256 seal. With one click, you can export a clean, executive PDF proving exactly which policies were enforced, when they were verified, and who approved them.

Q7

What is the ongoing cost and maintenance overhead of running this framework?

Virtually zero recurring software licensing cost. The entire package is built using open-standard, vendor-agnostic architecture. It runs on existing developer machines and CI/CD pipelines without requiring expensive multi-million dollar enterprise SaaS subscriptions.

Q8

Who owns and approves changes to company AI policies?

Policy governance is managed via a Git-based workflow owned by your company's AI Safety Review Board (typically comprising the Chief Risk Officer, Head of AI/Engineering, and Legal Counsel). Changes follow a standard Pull Request review process requiring multi-stakeholder approval before taking effect.
